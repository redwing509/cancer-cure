## Validation against exact formulas

The plan in the queue `JOBS.md` (20 Nov 2026) recorded an early check: 1-D slabs against the transfer-matrix method with max absolute error 0.0001 to 0.005 (fractions of incident power), and 3-D muscle spheres against Mie with 0.4 to 1.8% relative error in absorption cross-section. Those earlier bounds **do not hold for every finished job** in this snapshot. The figures below are copied from the finished result files.

Meep 1.35.1 was used for the slab and sphere FDTD jobs. Subpixel averaging (edge smoothing) is off, as documented in the plan: it produced NaN with ε ≈ 55 in tests, and staircasing is offset by resolution instead. That limitation is a plausible reason high-frequency slab errors are larger.

### Monte Carlo layer uncertainty (analytic, assumed widths)

Each `mc_uncertainty` job takes one of the 24 planar stacks and, at 60 frequencies from 100 MHz to 100 GHz, draws samples under **assumed** widths that are not taken from an opened source: a 10% (1σ, lognormal) factor on ε′ and ε″ of each tissue independently, and a 20% normal jitter on layer thickness. Polarization in these jobs: TE at 0°. Output percentiles are of peak SAR and of reflection `R`.

Finished in this snapshot:

- Box: 1085 jobs, each 150 samples per frequency, seeds from 100000 through 101132 (not a complete contiguous range; 1085 unique seeds). The planned box grid is 2000 seeds (`seed + 100000` for `range(2000)`). This family was still running (2 running, 48 pending in the 9:10:11 AM PST status).
- Rig1: 2995 jobs, each 1000 samples per frequency, unique seeds 0 through 2994 (block 2800–2994 has 195 of 200; the running batch was still writing). The original planned rig1 grid was 1000 seeds (0–999); later top-up batches extended the seed range. A pooled percentile table across the 2995 rig1 CSVs is `NOT FOUND` in this note, because those CSVs were not copied off rig1.

For the box jobs, [mc_uncertainty-box-median-across-seeds.csv](mc_uncertainty-box-median-across-seeds.csv) is a **derived** table: for each of the 24 stacks and each of 60 frequencies, the median across the finished seeds of that stack of `peak_sar_p50`, `peak_sar_p5`, `peak_sar_p95`, `R_p50`, `R_p5`, `R_p95`, and of (`p95` − `p5`). ## Known limits

- **MODEL-ONLY / not validated.** Do not read any number as a health, safety, or cancer-risk result.
- **Tissue phantoms, not a specific cancer.** No tumor was present in any job.
- **Head models are excluded** until further Gabriel Table 1 rows (grey/white matter, bone, CSF, blood) are opened and citation-checked. Sphere jobs are head-*size* or other chosen radii only.
- **Meep subpixel averaging (edge smoothing) is off.** High-frequency staircasing is a known source of the slab errors above 0.005.
- **Cell caps.** Jobs estimated above 1.2e7 cells (mail) or 6e6 cells (vps3) after symmetry were skipped by `refill` and never queued. Count from the original grid function: 10 skipped on mail (low-frequency small muscle spheres that need a fine mesh relative to the radius), 64 skipped on vps3 (low-frequency spheres and some low-frequency child-size spheroids). These are not failures.
- **openEMS** was not used (`NOT FOUND` in this result set).
- **PhysiCell** and other tumor-growth or pharmacology tools were not run and are **not vetted**. They remain candidates only.
- **Published SAR benchmarks** (IEEE/IEC flat phantom, Durney spheroid curves, and similar) were not compared, because those sources have not been opened and citation-checked for this note (`NOT FOUND`).
- **Layered-sphere Mie** comparison: `NOT FOUND`.
- **Density** is the conventional 1000 kg/m³. **Normalization** is 1 W/m² incident intensity.
- **MC widths** (10% on permittivity, 20% on thickness) are assumptions, not values from an opened uncertainty source.
- **Spheroid coverage is incomplete** for the child-size and teen-size tags, and some adult mid-grid frequencies were still running or pending on vps3.
- **Mail’s original refill grid is exhausted** (status `grid_remaining_est_core_h` was 0, `pending` was 0). Finished mail results already include later one-off top-up jobs (layered spheres at 3, 7.5, and 15 cm; mid-frequency homogeneous spheres; one teen-size spheroid; some child-size spheroids). Extending `refill.py` so the runner can top itself up is a separate code change, not part of this docs-only note.
- Estimates in `est_core_h` are rough.
