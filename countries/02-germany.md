### S3-Leitlinie Komplementärmedizin — statement 7.69 (survival)

- **Compound or therapy:** Mistelgesamtextrakt (*Viscum album* L.) (mistletoe whole extract)
- **Cancer type:** “Krebspatienten verschiedener Entitäten,” as named in the opened guideline (not one type)
- **Body site:** not stated as a single site in the opened guideline
- **Country:** Germany (Leitlinienprogramm Onkologie: AWMF, Deutsche Krebsgesellschaft, Deutsche Krebshilfe)
- **Study type:** Official S3 clinical practice guideline, Langversion 2.0, May 2024, AWMF register number 032-055OL. Printed label: “7.69 Evidenzbasiertes Statement 2020.”
- **Population:** The statement refers to “Krebspatienten verschiedener Entitäten,” as printed. No trial population was opened in this slice.
- **Measured result (quoted):** “Es liegen unterschiedliche Ergebnisse aus 3 Übersichtsarbeiten/Metaanalysen und einzelnen RCTs zur Wirksamkeit von Mistelgesamtextrakt (Viscum album L.) zur Verlängerung der Gesamtüberlebenszeit von Krebspatienten verschiedener Entitäten vor, die z.T. positive Ergebnisse, z.T. keine statistisch signifikante Wirksamkeit zeigen. Die Daten reichen für eine klare abschließende Bewertung nicht aus.” “Es kann keine Empfehlung für oder gegen eine Verordnung von Mistelgesamtextrakt (Viscum album L.) enthaltenden Präparaten mit dem Ziel der Verlängerung der Überlebenszeit gegeben werden.” (English translation: “There are differing results from 3 reviews/meta-analyses and individual RCTs on the efficacy of mistletoe whole extract (Viscum album L.) for prolonging overall survival of cancer patients of various entities, which in some cases show positive results and in some cases no statistically significant efficacy. The data are not sufficient for a clear final assessment.” “No recommendation can be given for or against prescribing preparations containing mistletoe whole extract (Viscum album L.) with the aim of prolonging survival time.”) The box is labeled Level of Evidence 1a and “Konsens.”
- **Link or DOI:** Official Langversion 2.0 PDF: https://www.leitlinienprogramm-onkologie.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Downloads/Leitlinien/Komplementär/Version_2/LL_Komplementärmedizin_Langversion_2.0.pdf — register page (live URL was an Anubis bot-check here; not used for quotes): https://register.awmf.org/de/leitlinien/detail/032-055OL
- **Evidence level:** not a primary study opened in this slice (official S3 guideline statement; the guideline itself prints Level of Evidence 1a)
- **Negative / null results:** The statement itself is a non-recommendation on survival and says the data are insufficient for a final assessment, with results described as partly positive and partly not statistically significant.
- **Source opened as:** full text (official Langversion 2.0 PDF, HTTP 200). The live AWMF register page was not usable here.

The guideline’s stated aim, in the same official PDF, is: “Ziel der S3-Leitlinie Komplementäre Onkologie ist es, Ärzten und weiterem in der Behandlung von Tumorpatienten einbezogenem Fachpersonal sowie den Patienten selbst evidenzbasierte und formal konsentierte Empfehlungen (und Negativ-Empfehlungen) für anstehende Entscheidungen zu geben.” (English translation: “The aim of the S3 guideline on complementary oncology is to give physicians and other specialist staff involved in the treatment of tumor patients, as well as the patients themselves, evidence-based and formally consented recommendations (and negative recommendations) for decisions to be made.”)

### Tröger et al. — randomized trial, advanced pancreatic cancer

- **Compound or therapy:** mistletoe extract, escalating subcutaneous doses three times a week, vs best supportive care alone
- **Cancer type:** locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic cancer (as named in the opened paper)
- **Body site:** pancreas
- **Country:** sponsor address in Freiburg, Germany (Clinical Research Dr. Tröger); trial conducted at the Clinical Centers of Serbia, Belgrade. This is a German-led trial, not a trial run in Germany.
- **Study type:** “An open, single-center, group-sequential, randomized phase III trial (ISRCTN70760582)”
- **Population:** 220 patients enrolled January 2009 to December 2010; QoL data “from 96 patients treated with mistletoe and 72 control patients.”
- **Measured result (quoted, English original):** “Those treated with mistletoe did better on all 6 functional scales and on 7 of 9 symptom scales, including pain (95% confidence interval [CI] -29 to –17), fatigue (95% CI –36.1 to –25.0), appetite loss (95% CI -51 to -36.7), and insomnia (95% CI –45.8 to –28.6).”
- **Limitations stated in the paper (quoted):** “The trial was intentionally not blinded … the patients' subjective reporting of their quality of life should be interpreted with a degree of caution, as it may be subject to distorting effects of various types”. The trial was stopped early after the interim survival analysis; the survival result is in a separate paper (reference 11 of the opened paper) that was not opened here, so no survival number is reported in this note.
- **Link:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4150026/
- **Evidence level:** RCT
- **Negative / null results:** none reported for QoL in the opened abstract; 2 of 9 symptom scales were not among those improved (the abstract says “7 of 9”).
- **Source opened as:** full text (PMC open copy).
- **Check note:** Hermes verify-S01 marked this quote FALSE because its extractor lowercased the text; the controller's opened copy matches the recorded quote, including capital “CI.”

### Schnell-Inderst et al. — HTA systematic review, breast cancer (GMS / DIMDI)

- **Compound or therapy:** Misteltherapie (MT) added to conventional cancer therapy
- **Cancer type:** Mammakarzinom (breast cancer) in women
- **Body site:** breast
- **Country:** German HTA programme (DIMDI, Cologne; HTA report no. 145, DOI 10.3205/hta000141L); authors at UMIT Tirol (Austria) and Medizinische Hochschule Hannover (Germany)
- **Study type:** systematic reviews underlying an HTA report (literature 2004 to October 2020); article DOI 10.3205/000312, published 14 Jul 2022
- **Measured result (quoted):** „Verbesserungen der gesundheitsbezogenen Lebensqualität gegenüber der Kontrollgruppe waren gering bis moderat. Aufgrund des hohen Verzerrungsrisikos in den Studien ist es möglich, dass der Unterschied nicht durch die MT bedingt ist.“ (English translation: “Improvements in health-related quality of life compared with the control group were small to moderate. Because of the high risk of bias in the studies, it is possible that the difference is not caused by mistletoe therapy.”)
- **Negative / null results (quoted):** „Eine Studie mit kleiner Fallzahl zeigte nach 5 Jahren keinen Effekt auf das progressionsfreie Überleben. Studien zum Effekt der MT auf das Gesamtüberleben fehlen.“ (English translation: “One study with a small sample size showed no effect on progression-free survival after 5 years. Studies on the effect of mistletoe therapy on overall survival are lacking.”)
- **Link:** https://journals.publisso.de/de/journals/gms/volume20/000312
- **Evidence level:** Systematic review
- **Source opened as:** full text (German page).
