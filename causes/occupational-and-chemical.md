## Question

## What the later evidence PR must gather

- Authoritative reviews and primary studies that support a causal role, with quoted measured results, populations, and evidence levels
- Null, negative, or weaker findings, including site-specific absences
- Whether the literature is about initiation, promotion, mortality, or only correlation
- How the candidate relates to age-adjusted trends, if the opened sources address trends

Prefer identified agents and occupations with measured exposures over generic statements about “chemicals.” Record both high-exposure occupational findings and environmental findings, and say which is which.
